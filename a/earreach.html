About EarReach

EarReach is a smart Bluetooth audio utility app that lets you stream live audio between your phone and compatible Bluetooth earbuds while also supporting audio recording. It offers a clean, professional interface designed for fast access, low latency, and an easy listening experience.

Whether you're listening to music, taking calls, or recording audio, EarReach delivers crystal-clear sound with minimal delay. The intuitive interface makes it easy to connect, control, and enjoy your audio experience.
