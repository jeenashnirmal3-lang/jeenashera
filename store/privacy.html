Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This policy details how we handle your digital footprint at Jeenash Store.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience, we collect minimal personal information necessary for authentication and transaction processing:

Full Name Email Address Phone Number IP Address Purchase History

2. How Your Data is Used

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your account via Google Firebase.
  • Providing access to your purchased digital bundles and courses.
  • Sending transaction receipts and critical product updates.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities and duplicate claims.

3. Security & Infrastructure

We utilize industry-standard cloud infrastructure to secure your data:

  • Authentication: Handled by Firebase Authentication (Google), ensuring your passwords are encrypted and never stored on our local servers.
  • Database: Your purchase records are stored in Firebase Firestore with strict security rules.
  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our store is encrypted via SSL (HTTPS).

4. Payment Processing

Jeenash Store does not collect or store financial sensitive information. All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Razorpay may collect your billing information as per their own privacy protocols to facilitate secure transactions.

5. Cookies Usage

We use essential cookies to keep you logged into your dashboard and to remember your preferences. These cookies do not track your activity on third-party websites. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some store features may stop functioning.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share information with our trusted service providers (Firebase and Razorpay) strictly for the purpose of operating our store and serving you.

7. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and data (Note: This will result in loss of access to purchased digital products).

8. Children's Privacy

Jeenash Store does not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete it from our systems.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Modified" date.

For data-related inquiries or deletion requests, contact us:
